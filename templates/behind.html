Meet the Founder & Creator of EduMate

Priyanshu Banerjee

Founder & Creator of EduMate

I am a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) student with an interest in technology, programming and artificial intelligence. EduMate is a project I started building to combine my interest in technology with a meaningful educational purpose.

The Beginning

EduMate started with a simple thought: Why should quality learning support be difficult to access?

Many students struggle with difficult topics. Some cannot afford private tuition, while others hesitate to ask questions in classrooms. I wanted to build a platform where students could learn at their own pace, ask questions freely and improve their understanding.

That idea became EduMate — Your AI Study Companion.

What Problem Does EduMate Solve?

EduMate aims to make learning more accessible and comfortable for students.

Expensive Tuition

Not every student can afford private tuition. EduMate aims to provide accessible learning assistance.

Difficulty Understanding

Students can upload study materials and ask AI to explain difficult topics in simpler language.

Fear of Asking Questions

Students can ask questions without worrying about embarrassment or being judged.

Lack of Practice

AI-generated quizzes help students practise and check their understanding.

Our Solution

EduMate brings useful AI-powered learning features together in one platform.

Students can upload PDFs, learn difficult concepts, ask doubts and generate quizzes to practise their knowledge.

The goal is to make learning simpler, more interactive and less stressful.

Future Vision

EduMate is just the beginning. My vision is to continue improving the platform by introducing more useful educational features, better learning experiences and tools that help students grow.

I want EduMate to become a platform where every student feels comfortable learning and exploring new ideas.

Our Mission

Making learning simpler, more accessible and less stressful for every student.
